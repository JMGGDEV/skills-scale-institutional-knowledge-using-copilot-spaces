## Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme projects begin with initiation, where the team defines the problem, SMART goal, success metrics, stakeholders, timeline, risks, and resource needs. After approval, planning turns the initiative into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, milestones, a Definition of Done, and identified dependencies.

During execution, teams deliver small, testable increments and track work on a project board through stages such as Backlog, Ready, In Progress, In Review, QA, and Done. Project Managers coordinate delivery, risks, schedules, and communications; Product Managers prioritize outcomes and measure impact; Developers build and test; QA validates acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input and approvals.

Communication is supported by standups, weekly delivery or PM/Product Manager syncs, milestone demos, and regular stakeholder updates. Risks and dependencies are recorded with owners and mitigation plans, reviewed throughout execution, and escalated when they could affect delivery or business outcomes.

Releases require completed acceptance criteria, passing CI and security checks, release notes, rollback planning, staging smoke tests, production verification, and stakeholder communication. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, or incidents capture lessons learned, assign improvement actions, and measure their impact so the process continues to evolve.
